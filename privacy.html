Privacy Policy

How the Dazen Development Resource Pack Converter handles files and browser data.

Last updated · October 7, 2026
⌂

Your resource pack is processed locally.

The converter is designed to read, scan, convert, preview, edit, and package selected resource files inside your browser. The application does not intentionally upload the contents of your resource pack to a Dazen Development conversion server.

01

Files you select

When you choose or drag a ZIP/MCPACK into the converter, JavaScript running in your browser reads the archive for validation and conversion. Generated results are created in the browser and are downloaded only when you choose to save them.

02

Local preview storage

Conversion previews, output archives, and manual mapping corrections may be stored temporarily in your browser using IndexedDB. This allows the separate Preview and Editor pages to access the conversion without sending the pack to an application backend.

Browser site data may be removed by your browser, private-browsing mode, storage limits, or manual clearing.

03

Static hosting and third-party delivery

The website may be served by GitHub Pages, and third-party CDN resources such as JSZip or js-yaml may be loaded by the page. Those infrastructure providers may receive ordinary web-request information such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps, and other data normally involved in delivering a website. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies.

04

External links

The site contains links to GitHub and the Dazen Development Discord community. Opening an external link takes you to that service, where its own privacy practices and account settings apply.

05

No resource-pack account required

The current converter does not require a Dazen Development user account to process a pack. No account login is needed for the local conversion workflow.

06

Security and limitations

Archive validation includes basic file-path and size safeguards, but no browser-based converter can guarantee that every third-party resource pack is safe, valid, or compatible. Only process files from sources you trust and review generated mappings before deploying them to a production server.

07

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as new utilities, hosting services, integrations, or server-side features are added. The date at the top of this page identifies the current published version.
